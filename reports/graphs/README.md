# Report graphs

Generated by `make plots` (`scripts/plot_report.py`) from `reports/benchmark.csv`, `gmu_published.csv`,
`profile.csv`, `synth_runs.csv`, `nks_study.csv`, the sweep and two profile VCDs. All numbers are on
xc7a100tftg256-2L (Vivado 2026.1, out of context, LWC wrapper included), the part GMU used.

Logan's SystemVerilog cores (`rtl/logan/src/`, `CORE=logan/*`, and with our options `CORE=merged/pipe*`, run through the same flow and wrapper) appear in 05, 07,
09, 11, 15 and 16, always as aqua squares (C3) with direct labels. Aqua was chosen over the next palette slot
(magenta) because magenta fails the normal-vision separation floor against the orange optimization path
(ΔE 12.9 < 15); blue / orange / aqua pass every check on all pairs.

Since 2026-11-23 the "2 rounds per stage" point (hollow orange diamond) is `brody/r2_BtoL` (J + the
handshake fixes K and L, 208.0 MHz), not r2 J. Logan's 20×2 ring with our options I and I + L
(`merged/pipe20x2d16_I` / `merged/pipe20x2d16_IL`, 245.2 / 249.2 MHz) are hollow aqua squares chained from his base
20×2 in 05, 11 and 15, bars in 07 and a row in 09 (I + L). M (no mask delay line, not adopted) is not plotted.
Added the same day: the 32-round ring with two entry points (`merged/pipe16x2d16_ILN`, 247.1 MHz, 5176 LUT,
1546 Kbps/LUT; chained after 20×2 + I + L, labeled "32-round ring") and Logan's 10×2 + our I, L
(`merged/pipe10x2d8_IL`, 244.1 MHz, 1244 Kbps/LUT; chained from his base 10×2). Graph 16 now compares our
r4 J and r2 L with his base 20×2 and the 32-round ring; graph 11 labels the four modified rings as one group
(244–249 MHz).

## Sources
- GMU values: Mohajerani et al., *SCA Evaluation and Benchmarking of Finalists in the NIST Lightweight
  Cryptography Standardization Process*, ePrint 2023/484 (`docs/SCA-Benchmarking.pdf`), Tables 12 and 14,
  unprotected implementations. `reports/gmu_published.csv` is parsed from the PDF text, not retyped.
  Elephant_GMU is GMU's own Elephant (see `rtl/brody/base_r1/README.md` for how it compares with ours).
- Cycle-accurate numbers come from Questa runs of the LWC testbench (`make cycles`, `make profile`).

## Regenerating
- matplotlib is not in the system Python. Install it somewhere and point `PYTHONPATH` at it, e.g.
  `python3 -m pip install --target /tmp/pylib matplotlib`, then `PYTHONPATH=/tmp/pylib make plots`.
- Figure 13 needs the r4_BtoH profile VCDs with the issue signals added. Make the do-file with
  `python3 scripts/profile.py do --core brody/r4_BtoH --out build/prof/calls.do`, then add these lines
  before `run -all`:
  ```
  vcd add /LWC_TB/uut/Inst_Cipher/b_v
  vcd add /LWC_TB/uut/Inst_Cipher/b_p
  vcd add /LWC_TB/uut/Inst_Cipher/b_kind
  ```
  Then run, for WL in short and long:
  `make sim CORE=brody/r4_BtoH SET=prof_$WL SIM=questa VSIM_FLAGS=-voptargs=+acc VSIM_DO="do $PWD/build/prof/calls.do"`.
  Without them the figure is skipped.
- `reports/nks_study.csv` holds hand-measured points (NKS edited, `make cycles` rerun); see
  `rtl/brody/r4_BtoG/README.md` and `rtl/brody/r2_BtoH/README.md`.
